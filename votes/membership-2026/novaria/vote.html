Introducing Novaria and Her Daughters

Today, we present for your prayerful consideration, a petition from Novaria, who is a 27 years old mother of two daughters. She comes before this Family not as a stranger, but as one who has studied our ways, engaged with our faith, and now seeks to complete her journey home.

Novaria asks to be accepted into the Reich Family, to take our name as her own in an act of sacred transformation, commitment, and pride. She does not ask lightly. She seeks to become your sister in fellowship, your mother in service, and your daughter in learning and faith.

A woman of order and diligence by profession, Novaria has worked as an accountant. She now feels a deeper calling; to transplant her life from her current home to Europe with her young family, to sink roots into our shared soil, and to contribute her skills and steadfast heart to our collective well-being.

She brings with her two precious daughters, Rosavia (6) and Zeranya (3). They are not merely accompanying her. They, too, have expressed a child's pure desire to enter the Family, to bear its name, and to grow within its embrace. Novaria wishes you to know that the girls are intelligent, well-mannered, and already hold a love for the Holy Trinity in their young hearts. They arrive with open minds, ready to be one of the Reich Family children.

Novaria's commitment is clear and profound. She declares her intention to fully embrace our Trinitarian religion as her own, to live by the Commandments in both letter and spirit, and to dedicate herself and her family to a life that is wholesome, healthy, and holy.

You have reviewed her interview and her file. You have seen the contours of her past and the sincerity of her intent. Now, we ask you to discern with your heart and spirit. Is Novaria, with her daughters Rosavia and Zeranya, a good and faithful match for this Family? Does her petition reflect the genuine calling we pray for in new members? Vote based on the interview and other information provided to you. Vote with a reliance on the Knowledge and Wisdom of the Holy Spirit. May the Holy Trinity guide your vote.
